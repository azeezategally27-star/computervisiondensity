Mauritius Airport Operations — Overview

Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU) operations consider local weather patterns (trade winds), runway occupancy patterns, and typical international hub connections. Key points:
- Peak arrivals: mornings 06:00-09:00 and evenings 17:00-20:00.
- Gate reassignment should avoid mixing widebody and narrowbody during peak to reduce tow conflicts.
- Ground transport (buses) must be scheduled when remote stands are used.
