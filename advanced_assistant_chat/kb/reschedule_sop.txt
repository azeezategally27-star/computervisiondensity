SOP: Flight Rescheduling

When a flight requires rescheduling due to operational constraints (weather, crew, technical), follow these steps:
1. Check current gate occupancy and future gate assignments.
2. Verify crew duty time and availability.
3. Check passenger connectivity & inform impacted passengers immediately.
4. Coordinate with ground handling for baggage and catering changes.
5. Update the operational system (AODB) and log the change with reason.

Example: If delaying MK123 by 45 minutes, reserve ground crew for an extra hour and update inbound baggage operations.
