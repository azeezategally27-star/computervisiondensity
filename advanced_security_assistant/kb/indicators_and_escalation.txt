Indicators and escalation — screening guidance

This document contains non-actionable, high-level indicators and escalation guidance intended for screening staff and system demonstrations. It is for training and awareness only.

Indicators (non-exhaustive, non-actionable)
- Unlabeled dense objects in rigid containers that do not match declared contents.
- Significant mismatch between declared baggage weight and scan-estimated weight.
- Concealment behavior (e.g., frequent avoidance of screening lines, abnormal patterns at checkpoints).

Escalation & safe handling
- If screening detects an item of concern: isolate the baggage in a secure area and notify the security supervisor immediately.
- Do not attempt to open suspicious items with untrained personnel. Use non-destructive inspection first (X-ray review by trained operator).
- Record incident metadata: entity id, time, location, visual notes, and photos if policy permits.
- Follow local standard operating procedures and coordinate with law enforcement as required.
