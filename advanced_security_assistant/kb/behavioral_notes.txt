Behavioral observation notes (for staff training)

This doc provides high-level behavioral patterns that may be useful for staff training. It is not an operational manual or a blueprint for interdiction.

Examples of concerning patterns:
- Attempts to evade standard passenger processing (splitting luggage between multiple handlers, frequent rerouting).
- Unaccompanied bags left near public areas for prolonged periods.

Response: observe, notify, and escalate per the local SOPs.
